About the Workshop

Exploring emerging directions at the intersection of mathematics, computation, and data.

The workshop focuses on current developments and future directions in optimization, game theory, algorithms, artificial intelligence, and data science. Through invited talks and discussions, the programme highlights mathematical and computational approaches to complex decision-making and real-world social and technological systems.

Optimization

Models and methods for efficient decision-making in complex systems.

Game Theory & Algorithms

Strategic interaction, information, and algorithmic perspectives on decision problems.

AI & Data Science

Data-driven intelligence and predictive approaches for societal and practical applications.
